Routing

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Fornax Labs?

Fornax Labs is an agentic AI platform for advisory and professional firms: mutual fund distributors, insurance brokers, CA and tax firms, and law firms. Our agents pick up client requests from email, WhatsApp and web forms, work out what is needed, do the routine work, and bring in a person for anything sensitive. Every run is tracked end to end.

What is agent orchestration?

Agent orchestration is the work of running AI agents reliably inside a business: connecting them to the right channels, tools and data, sequencing their steps, routing sensitive actions to a person for approval, and recording every run so you can see what happened and what it cost. Fornax Labs handles that layer so teams get working agents rather than a blank canvas.

Which channels and tools do Fornax agents work with?

Agents take work in from email, WhatsApp, web forms and the systems your firm already uses, such as practice-management software, CRMs and back-office platforms. Email is live today, and WhatsApp and further channels are rolling out with our pilot customers.

What kinds of work can Fornax agents do?

Each industry has a ready-made playbook. For fund distributors: SIP changes, redemptions, KYC and nominee updates, statements and complaints. For insurance brokers: quotes, renewals, claims and endorsements. For CA and tax firms: filing requests, tax notices, document collection and fee follow-ups. For law firms: new matter intake, hearing updates, document requests and client updates. New kinds of request can be added to any playbook.

What is Sugamkar?

Sugamkar is the first product on the Fornax platform. It reads client messages from email, WhatsApp and other channels, recognises what each request is, and turns it into a tracked task on one worklist. It is open for pilots with mutual fund distributors.

How do approvals and tracking work?

Sensitive steps, such as sending a reply to a client or changing a record, are routed to the right person for approval before they happen. Every step, tool call and decision is logged and searchable, and each agent shows time saved, success rate and spend.

Can I customise rules, routing and approvals?

Yes. You set the conditions an agent acts on, such as the kind of request, an amount, the client or the channel. You decide where the work goes: a person, a team or round robin. And you choose which actions need approval, from whom and above what limit. No code required.

Do I need an engineering team to use Fornax?

No. You start from a ready-made playbook for your industry, connect your channels and tools, and set the rules and limits. Fornax Labs is built for operators and small teams, not developers.
