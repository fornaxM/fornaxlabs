Why we exist

Subscription billing wasn't built for this.

Four problems we hear from every AI-native team in their first meeting with us. ConsumIQ is the answer to all four.

01

Pricing changed. Billing didn't.

Your product charges by tokens, calls, agent-runs, or compute-seconds — but you're still stitching together a metering vendor, an entitlements layer, and an invoicing system. Three sources of truth. Four reconciliation tickets per close.

02

Margins move by the minute.

A single account can flip from 60% margin to negative in an afternoon when a customer's usage pattern changes. If you can't see per-customer cost-to-serve in real time, you're flying blind on the metric that decides whether you're a business.

03

The buyer wants the other side.

Your customers' finance teams need cross-vendor spend visibility. The vendor that gives it to them — without forcing a procurement project — becomes the one they can't switch off.

04

One platform, one schema.

Event capture, rating, entitlements, invoicing, and the intelligence on top — all in a single source of truth. Built so the integration that takes six months elsewhere takes six weeks here.
